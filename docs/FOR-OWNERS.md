# Switchboard for business owners

Switchboard is one screen on your computer. When the assistant that does work
for you (writing replies, sending quotes, checking on jobs) needs a yes, a
choice or a look from you, it puts that on this screen instead of waiting in a
chat. You tap, and it carries on.

## Opening the board

- Whoever set it up opened it in your browser once. Bookmark that page.
- If you lose it, ask your assistant to "open my Switchboard". It opens the page
  in your browser for you.
- Open it through that link once in each browser you use. A browser that did
  not come through the link can look but cannot approve anything.

## What the colors mean

| Color | What it means |
|---|---|
| Gold | Needs you. Read it and tap an answer. |
| Gold ring | You said yes. Your assistant has not reported it done yet. |
| Blue | Sent, waiting on someone else. If they go quiet, it comes back gold. |
| Green | Something running by itself, fine. |
| Red | Something broke. The fix is usually attached. |
| Dim | Lined up for later, or asleep until a date. |

When nothing is gold, the screen says "0 lights need you right now." That is
the goal.

## Answering

- **Approve.** The words on the screen are exactly the words that go out. If
  they change after you open them, the buttons stop and the row says
  "Look again".
- **Change…** Fix the words yourself, or write what you want different and
  your assistant brings back a new version for you to approve.
- **Drop it.** Say no. Nothing goes out, and your assistant is told.
- **Snooze.** Not today. It comes back tomorrow morning.

## Undo

Every tap waits a moment before your assistant can act on it: 20 seconds, or
60 for a message to a person. The "Just now" strip lists each tap with an Undo
button. An approval your assistant has not picked up yet can be taken back
from its row at any time.

## Nothing goes out without your tap

Switchboard never sends anything itself, and your assistant cannot approve for
you. Messages to people, money, public posts and changes to your live website
wait for your tap. After you approve, your assistant does the sending and
reports back "sent", "failed" or "not sure". If it is not sure, you are asked
before anything is sent again.

## Reach out?

Ideas you did not ask for, like texting an old customer, stay out of your way.
They wait folded in one drawer, "Reach out?", with the person,
when you last talked and the words ready. Open it when you want to, and either
Copy & open (then "Yes, it went") or "Not now". It never rings and never counts
against you.

## Banners and texts

At the bottom of the board are two switches: "Banners on this computer" and
"Texts to your phone". Both start the way you chose during setup. Flip either
one at any time.

## Who to ask for help

- First, whoever set Switchboard up for you.
- Or your assistant: say "something is wrong with my Switchboard" and describe
  what you see.
- Or open an issue at
  [github.com/ZCOLLINSKY/switchboard/issues](https://github.com/ZCOLLINSKY/switchboard/issues)
  with what you were doing and where you got stuck. Leave out customer names
  and numbers.
